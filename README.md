## How to Choose the Right Anti-Static Foam Rolls

Choosing foam based only on its color or thickness is not sufficient. The electrical properties and physical characteristics must match the packaging application.

1. **Identify the Electronic Product:** Determine whether you are packaging circuit boards, sensors, connectors, or other sensitive components.
2. **Understand ESD Requirements:** Review the level of static control required for the specific product.
3. **Select the Foam Type:** Compare anti-static, static-dissipative, and conductive foam according to the application.
4. **Check Electrical Specifications:** Request surface or volume resistivity information and relevant test methods.
5. **Review Foam Thickness:** Select a suitable thickness based on the cushioning and separation requirements.
6. **Consider Density:** Evaluate the foam's compression and support characteristics in relation to product weight.
7. **Determine Roll Dimensions:** Specify the required width, length, and thickness for your packing process.
8. **Review Documentation:** Request technical data sheets and applicable test reports before using the material for sensitive electronic products.

**Important:** Anti-static, static-dissipative, and conductive foam are not interchangeable. Ordinary anti-static foam should not automatically be treated as conductive or static-shielding packaging. Confirm the material's electrical specifications and use it as part of an appropriate ESD-protective packaging system.
